Three kinds of ticket

Decidedtickets cover breakdowns and jobs, IT included Proposedthe raiser picks the kind with one tap

Stopped

The machine or service is down.

  • Downtime clock runs
  • Top priority; timers apply
Running with a fault

Still working, but not right.

  • No downtime unless it stops
  • Timers apply
Job

Install, change or IT work: a switchboard, a fan, a PC.

  • No downtime
  • Incharge decides; sets a target date
